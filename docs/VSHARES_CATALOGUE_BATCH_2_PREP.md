# VSHARES — Batch 2 preparation: Productivity

- Date: 2026-11-14 · Status: **candidates only. Not written up, not seeded, not published.**

Batch 1 has no Productivity card. These are the candidates for that gap,
held at the planning stage deliberately: the remaining cards are not written
until the house style in batch 1 is approved, so corrections there shape
everything that follows rather than having to be applied ninety times.

## Candidate sources

Chosen to avoid the two failure modes that dominate productivity writing:
personality-driven advice with no evidence, and systems that take longer to
maintain than the work they organise. **None of these links has been
verified** — this environment has no network access.

| Topic | Candidate source | Why it earns a place |
|---|---|---|
| Attention and focus | Research summaries on interruption cost from a university or public-health body | The cost of switching is measurable, which makes it teachable rather than a matter of opinion |
| Planning and prioritising | A public-sector or academic guide to prioritisation under constraint | Avoids commercial frameworks that exist to sell a tool |
| Habits | A behavioural-science explainer from a university or health body | Habit advice is unusually prone to confident claims with thin evidence |
| Note taking | A practical guide to writing notes you reopen | Pairs with the spaced-repetition card already in batch 1 |
| Meetings and email | Guidance on running meetings that produce decisions | A concrete organisational skill with an immediate next step |

## What each card will have to clear

The checklist in `VSHARES_EDITORIAL_CHECKLIST.md`, and in particular:

- The next step must be doable this week, not a change of personality.
- No card may claim a productivity gain that the source does not support.
- Nothing recommending a paid tool as the main point of the card.
