## How these were produced

Twenty candidate drafts (`src/factory/originals/batch2.ts`) were run through the
existing Content Factory (`runPipeline`), deduplicated against the ten seeded
batch-1 cards (`classifyDuplicate`), and checked for bridge-readiness. **No
supporting quotes were supplied and no live retrieval was run in this
environment**, so the factory left the evidence of every candidate UNVERIFIED by
design. Provenance (source URL + publisher) is *proposed* from reputable
publishers; it has not been retrieved or confirmed. This is deliberate: the
factory never fabricates verification, and the rubric penalises unsupported
claims.

structural blocker; it is **not** verification, **not** approval, and **not**
clearance to publish. All six still carry unverified evidence and must be grounded
and human-reviewed. The fourteen `reject` candidates are rejected precisely
because their claims are not yet supported — they need evidence (T4 or a
`FACTORY_LIVE=1` retrieval) before they could pass, and we did **not** lower the
bar or invent support to reach twenty.

`spotting-investment-scams`, `reviewing-your-credit-report`): each would need a
**second, independent** authenticated reviewer before any approval, and none may
be self-approved.

## What has NOT happened

- No card was inserted into any database (hosted or local) by this task.
- No card is published, approved, link-checked or verified.
- No citation, quote or fact-check result was fabricated.
- Topic UUIDs are **not** resolved here; importing a candidate as a draft (via
  the moderator-only `create_card`) is a later, owner-run step that resolves the
  topic from taxonomy and leaves the card as an unchecked draft (proven by
  `supabase/tests/cases/300_factory_bridge_draft_only.sql`).
